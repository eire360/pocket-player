# Pocket Player

Modeled iPod-style music controller; Mac and iPhone versions.

## Download

[Download source ZIP](https://github.com/eire360/pocket-player/archive/refs/heads/main.zip)

## Setup

Requires an Apple Silicon Mac with macOS 14 or later and Apple developer command-line tools.

```sh
./build.sh
open "Pocket Player.app"
```

Open the Music app, then choose Connect Apple Music in Pocket Player. Allow Music automation when macOS asks.

## Controls

Scroll or drag the wheel to navigate. Center/Enter selects, Menu/Backspace goes back, Space toggles playback, and the side buttons skip tracks.
